# v0.1.0 · 2026-10-23

First release of KoPlay: Spotify on your KOReader e-reader.

**What it does**
- Now playing screen: big album art, title / artist / album, progress, previous / play-pause / next
- Controls while reading: play/pause, next and previous as KOReader gestures, with a short notification
- KoPlay menu (Tools → KoPlay): current track and controls right in the menu
- Library: playlists, liked songs, search, recently played, queue; long-press a track to add it to the queue
- Device picker when no Spotify device is active
- English and Turkish, following KOReader's language

**Install**
1. Unzip `koplay.koplugin.zip` into `koreader/plugins/` and restart KOReader.
2. Create a Spotify developer app and run `koplay_auth.py` once on your computer. Step-by-step instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/orkunseyman/koplay#installation).

Requires Spotify Premium. Tested on a Kindle Paperwhite 3 with KOReader v2026.07. Reports from Kobo and other devices are welcome.
