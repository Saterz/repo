# v1.3.0 · 2026-10-24

## Reading Log for Simple UI 1.3.0

A Cute style, a cover calendar, more quote cards, and faster redraws. 

### New cards
- **Cover calendar**: every day of the month with the cover of the book you read most that day, with its own Dashboard tab, **Calendar**. Tap a day for its books; **Today** jumps back to this month.
- **Week in covers**: this week's covers in one row, as a card or in the Mini dashboard.
- **Quote of the day 2, 3 and 4**: each shows a random highlight of its own, never the same one as your other quote cards.

### Style: Default or Cute
Long-press the Dashboard (or Look and colours on any card) > **Style** > **Cute** turns on every cute look at once:
- Hearts instead of rings
- A peony for the Monthly challenge
- A garden for the Book club
- A jar of hearts for the Year goal
- Notebook paper for the quote cards
- Polaroids for the cover calendars
- A scrapbook for Continue reading
- Sticky notes for Reading trends
- Book spines and hearts for the charts
- A sky for Today's timeline
- A desk calendar for the Finish forecast

### Also
- **Wallpaper**: single cards over a see-through wallpaper are drawn as solid cards, like the Dashboard's, instead of putting text straight onto the picture.
- Control center buttons can show a PNG or JPEG of your own
- **Faster**: cards that haven't changed are copied instead of redrawn, and a tab draws whole in one go.



**Install:** Tools > Reading Log > Check for updates, or replace the `readinglog.koplugin` folder and restart. Your settings are kept.

# v1.2.0 · 2026-10-22

# Reading Log for Simple UI 1.2.0

## New

- **Four new languages: Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian.** The cards, the Dashboard and all their settings follow the language KOReader is showing.

- **Wallpaper and transparency.** With a Simple UI wallpaper on, the Dashboard follows its **Background Opacity** . The date, the time and the page titles at the top get a framed plate behind them, like the tab bar, so they stay readable on any wallpaper. Without a wallpaper they look as before.
- **Check for updates.** KOReader menu > Tools > Reading Log > **Check for updates** looks for a newer release, shows what's new, and installs it when you say so. Restart KOReader afterwards; your settings are kept. 

## Improved

- **Tab names** that are too long for their tab (in another language, or a name of your own) get smaller to fit instead of being cut off.
- **Today's timeline** follows KOReader's 12/24-hour clock setting.



## Thanks

- [@DevGizmo86](https://github.com/DevGizmo86) for the translation system and the Italian translation, first made for Reading Log for Bookshelf.

## Updating

Replace the `readinglog.koplugin` folder and restart KOReader. Your settings are kept. From this version on, you can update from the Reading Log menu instead.

# v1.1.0 · 2026-10-20


### New in 1.1

- **Reading trends**: your last 90 days against your year (time, pages, reading days, session length, speed, books finished).
- **Monthly challenge**: one goal for the month, picked from your last three months or set by you.
- **Finish forecast**: when you'll finish the books you're reading at your recent pace, and where your year is heading.
- **This week vs last**: your average a day this week against last week (or month, or year).
- **Today's timeline**: each sitting today on a line through the day.
- **Control centre**: up to 8 buttons for any action, with brightness and warmth.
- **Library stats**, **Most-read authors**, **Random pick** (only the next book of a series) and **Quote of the day** (from your own highlights).
- **Mini dashboard**: one row of up to three small cards for the top of the home screen.
- **Dashboard**: Trends and Library pages, up to 7 tabs, move tabs, remove pages you added, reset the whole Dashboard.
- **Text size** for every card and for the whole Dashboard.
- **Finish dates**: a book reopened for a moment no longer moves to today's date.
